# Domain Glossary

## POS & Order Intake
* **Order:** A ticket representing a collection of requested menu items for a customer.
* **Order Type:** The fulfillment mode, which can be `Dine-In`, `Takeaway`, or `Delivery`.
* **KOT (Kitchen Order Ticket):** The list of items prepared by the kitchen for an order.
* **Menu Item:** An item listed on the menu with a price, availability status, and category.

## Table Management
* **Dine-In Session:** An active dining event associated with a physical table, containing customer info, order reference, and elapsed time.
* **Table:** A physical seating unit in the restaurant with a specific capacity and status (`Available`, `Occupied`, or `Dirty`).

## Billing & Settlement
* **Bill:** The final invoice of an order to be settled by the customer.
* **Payment Type:** The payment mode used for settlement: `Cash`, `UPI`, or `Card`.

## Inventory & Overhead
* **Inventory Item:** Raw ingredients tracked by quantity and units (e.g. kg, ltr, pcs).
* **Supplier:** A vendor supplying inventory stock.
* **Expense:** Overhead costs like rent, salaries, utility bills, or ingredient purchases.
